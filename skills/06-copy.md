# 06 — Copy & Credibility (25 Skills)

## 1. Generic Headlines
**Slop:** “Elevate your workflow”, “Build the future of work”, “Unlock your potential”, “Supercharge your team”.  
**Why:** Could be for any product.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Specific outcome for a specific person. “Close your books in 2 days” beats “Streamline your finances”.

## 2. Buzzword Density
**Slop:** seamless, powerful, stunning, next-level, cutting-edge, world-class, effortless, revolutionary.  
**Why:** Empty language.  
**When OK:** Extremely rare, ironic, or precise use.  
**Fix:** Replace with concrete benefits and facts.

## 3. Em Dash Overuse in Copy
**Slop:** Multiple em dashes per paragraph.  
**Why:** Strong AI writing tell.  
**When OK:** Proper punctuation.  
**Fix:** Use periods and commas.

## 4. Everything in Threes
**Slop:** “Fast. Simple. Powerful.” or three parallel benefits.  
**Why:** Formulaic.  
**When OK:** When three is the natural number.  
**Fix:** Vary structure. Use specific numbers and details.

## 5. Fake Social Proof
**Slop:** Round numbers (10x, 500+), generic company logos, testimonials with first name + initial and AI headshots.  
**Why:** Users have learned to ignore them.  
**When OK:** Real, verifiable proof.  
**Fix:** Real names, roles, photos, and links. Or omit the section.

## 6. Emoji as Bullets or Icons
**Slop:** ✅ 🚀 💡 as list markers.  
**Why:** Inconsistent across devices and looks informal/AI.  
**When OK:** Playful brands.  
**Fix:** Use proper icons or simple bullets.

## 7. “It’s not just X. It’s Y.” Structure
**Slop:** Repeated antithesis sentences.  
**Why:** AI writing pattern.  
**When OK:** Once, if clever.  
**Fix:** Direct statements.

## 8. Vague CTAs
**Slop:** “Get Started”, “Learn More”, “Try it Free” with no context.  
**Why:** Low conversion and unclear.  
**When OK:** Clear context elsewhere.  
**Fix:** Specific: “Start free trial”, “See pricing”, “Book a demo”.

## 9. Feature Lists Instead of Benefits
**Slop:** Long noun-heavy feature dumps.  
**Why:** Users care about outcomes.  
**When OK:** Technical docs.  
**Fix:** Lead with the result, then the feature.

## 10. Over-Promising
**Slop:** “The only tool you’ll ever need”, “10x productivity guaranteed”.  
**Why:** Unbelievable.  
**When OK:** Never without proof.  
**Fix:** Honest, specific claims.

## 11. Placeholder Copy Left In
**Slop:** “Lorem ipsum”, “Your awesome feature”, “Coming soon”.  
**Why:** Unfinished.  
**When OK:** During development only.  
**Fix:** Real copy or remove the section.

## 12. All-Caps Shouting
**Slop:** Headlines or buttons in all caps.  
**Why:** Hard to read and aggressive.  
**When OK:** Very short labels.  
**Fix:** Sentence or title case.

## 13. Missing Specificity
**Slop:** “Save time”, “Improve efficiency”.  
**Why:** Meaningless without numbers or context.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** “Save 4 hours per week on invoicing” or similar.

## 14. Stock Testimonial Language
**Slop:** “Game changer”, “Couldn’t live without it”, “Exceeded expectations”.  
**Why:** Generic.  
**When OK:** Real quotes that happen to use those words.  
**Fix:** Specific results and natural language.

## 15. No Clear Audience
**Slop:** Copy that tries to speak to everyone.  
**Why:** Resonates with no one.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Write to a specific person or role.

## 16. Passive Voice Overuse
**Slop:** “Features are provided”, “Results can be achieved”.  
**Why:** Weak and distant.  
**When OK:** Rare.  
**Fix:** Active voice. “You close books in 2 days”.

## 17. Jargon Without Explanation
**Slop:** Industry terms with no context.  
**Why:** Excludes users.  
**When OK:** Expert audiences.  
**Fix:** Plain language or explain on first use.

## 18. Inconsistent Tone
**Slop:** Formal in one section, casual in another.  
**Why:** Unprofessional.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Consistent brand voice.

## 19. Missing Value Proposition
**Slop:** No clear statement of what the product does and for whom.  
**Why:** Users leave.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Clear value prop above the fold.

## 20. Overly Long Paragraphs
**Slop:** Walls of text.  
**Why:** Hard to scan.  
**When OK:** Never on web.  
**Fix:** Short paragraphs, bullets, scannable structure.

## 21. Fake Urgency
**Slop:** “Limited time”, “Only 3 spots left” without truth.  
**Why:** Destroys trust.  
**When OK:** Real scarcity.  
**Fix:** Honest messaging.

## 22. Missing Social Proof When Available
**Slop:** No testimonials, logos, or numbers when they exist.  
**Why:** Missed opportunity.  
**When OK:** Early stage.  
**Fix:** Use real proof.

## 23. Inconsistent Terminology
**Slop:** Same feature called different names.  
**Why:** Confusing.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Consistent naming.

## 24. Weak Benefit Statements
**Slop:** Features described without “so that” or outcome.  
**Why:** Users don’t care about features alone.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** Always connect to user outcome.

## 25. No Call to Action Clarity
**Slop:** Multiple competing CTAs or none.  
**Why:** Decision paralysis.  
**When OK:** Never.  
**Fix:** One primary CTA per section or page.
